Why the wait?

Three forces, all pulling at once:

  • The vendor needs visibility. In June 2026 Anthropic designated its Mythos-class models “Covered Models,” requiring 30-day retention of prompts and outputs on every platform — including Bedrock with ZDR — because misuse like best-of-N jailbreaking and agentic espionage is only visible across many requests. Microsoft restricted internal Fable 5 use in response.
  • Regulated customers need minimization. Finance, healthcare, and legal buyers told Anthropic the models were unadoptable under their data governance — so on September 2, 2026 it reversed course: Enterprise Frontier Safeguards put monitoring data in customer-controlled infrastructure with customer keys, and Fable 5.1 shipped ZDR-eligible. Whether 5.1 clears compliance faster than Fable 5 is now the experiment to watch on this page.
  • The process itself is slow and batched. Traditional FedRAMP authorization runs 12–18 months per package, and models accrete in batches — e.g. May 2025, four models at once for GovCloud High. The FedRAMP 20x reform aims pilot authorizations in under two months.
